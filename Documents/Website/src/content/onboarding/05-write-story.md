# Write the Story

Where the story comes from and the rules every line follows.

1. The chapter scripts (PDF) are in `Documents/Story Specs/`. Every episode follows its spec.
2. Follow the [Writing Guidelines](/#story-tone). The language is neutral and non-triggering, with no blame and no graphic detail. The family is Mom, Dad and Tim.
3. The player's chosen name replaces "Joey" in dialogue, and each profile has its own saves.
4. Where the words are written: Chapter 4 and later in cinematics and conversation assets ([Create a conversation](#create-conversation)); Chapters 1–3 in scripts, editable in the Inspector through **Dialogue Words** ([Edit the words of Chapters 1–3](#edit-words)).
5. After every episode, the reflection journal and the self-assessment run (`EpisodeReflection`).
