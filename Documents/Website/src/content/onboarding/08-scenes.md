# How Scenes Work

Each episode is one Unity scene. The scenes are edited by hand in Unity.

The episode scenes were first generated by chapter builders. **They are now the source of truth and are edited directly in Unity.** The builders are kept under **Mindscape › Legacy Builders** only for reference: running one regenerates its scenes from code and throws away every hand-made change, so each one asks before it runs. To make something new, use **Mindscape › Story › New Episode…** ([Create an episode](#create-episode)).

The game ships 22 scenes (**File › Build Profiles**): Intro, main_menu_new, Tutorial, Ch1 1–4, Cutscene – Episode 1, Ch2 1–5, MinigameTestLab, Ch3 1–8.

## What is in an episode scene

| Object | What it is |
|---|---|
| The episode object (named after the chapter, for example *Chapter 3 - The Corner of the Room*) | Holds the story component, its spots, start point and camera views, and the episode's people and props |
| Player | Joey: movement, camera, outfit. Its movement and camera values come from a [Player Settings](#player-settings) asset |
| Main Camera | The follow camera; story shots move it |
| World, House And Lot / school grounds | The shared city and the building the episode happens in |
| Ambient Life, Street Life, City Traffic | Walkers, joggers, the family in the house, cars and buses |
| Story Markers (inside the episode object) | [Positions you can drag](#story-markers) that the code would otherwise work out |
